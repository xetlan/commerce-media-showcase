What DXA does NOT do well here — be honest

This page pushed DXA to its limit. Below that limit are real gaps PA fills.
  • No cross-device identity stitching — DXA is session-anchored. A user who exposes on mobile and converts on desktop is two unrelated events. The 18% of conversions that span devices in the PA view are lost here.
  • No behavioral cohort definition from event-property combinations — segments are URL-pattern, click-zone, or attribute-based. "Users with ≥3 hero exposures and 0 ATCs in 14d" is awkward at best.
  • No audience destinations — cohorts can be exported as segments for use inside DXA, but no native sync to Meta / Google / CRM / Klaviyo.
  • No multi-session retention curves — DXA reports session-bound metrics; "do exposed users return more often" is not natively answered.
  • No A/B variant analysis without explicit instrumentation — variant tags must be added as a custom segment dimension; flexibility is lower than PA's property-on-every-event model.
  • No multi-touch attribution models — DXA's Impact Quantification is segment-vs-segment lift, not first-touch / linear / time-decay attribution comparison.
  • No predictive propensity scoring — Sense surfaces patterns retrospectively, doesn't score individual users for forward-looking action.
